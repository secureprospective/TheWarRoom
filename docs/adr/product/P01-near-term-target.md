# The near-term target: one real 32-team league, and trusted leagues never put at risk

Status: Accepted by Christopher
Date: 2026-11-22

**Decision.** Through 2027, success means one real 32-team league plays a full season on TheWarRoom. The leagues that trust us (Legacy first among them) are never put at risk.

**Why.** 32 teams is the hardest case; smaller leagues follow easily. Trust can't be rebuilt once lost.

**What it means for agents.** Any change that could hurt a real league's data is stopped and brought to Christopher first.
