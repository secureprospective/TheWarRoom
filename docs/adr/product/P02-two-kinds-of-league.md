# Two kinds of league: MFL leagues and native leagues

Status: Accepted by Christopher
Date: 2026-11-22

**Decision.** In an MFL league, MFL is the official record; TheWarRoom is a companion that reads it, adds tools, and never writes back. In a native league, TheWarRoom is the record.

**Why.** Legacy may stay on MFL for years, and that is fine.

**What it means for agents.** Code that serves MFL leagues is current, not leftover. A league moves to native only through the import (cutover), and only when its people choose to.
