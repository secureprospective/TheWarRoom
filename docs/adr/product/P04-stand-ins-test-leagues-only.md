# AI Stand-ins play in test leagues only

Status: Accepted by Christopher
Date: 2026-11-22

**Decision.** A Stand-in (an AI that holds an empty team and plays it like an owner: lineups, bids, trades) plays only in test leagues for now.

**Why.** We don't yet know whether real people will feel an AI's trades are fair.

**What it means for agents.** No Stand-in in a real league until Christopher rules on it. Test leagues of many shapes use Stand-ins freely.
