# Real player photos yes; NFL logos and betting odds no

Status: Accepted by Christopher
Date: 2026-11-22

**Decision.** Real player photos are allowed. NFL logos and betting odds are not.

**Why.** Photos make the app feel alive. Logos bring legal trouble, and betting is not what this game is about.

**What it means for agents.** No logo artwork and no odds anywhere on screen; win-chance bars are fine.
