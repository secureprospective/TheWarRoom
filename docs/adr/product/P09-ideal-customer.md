# Our ideal customer: the big-league dynasty commissioner

Status: Accepted by Christopher
Date: 2026-11-22

**Decision.** The primary customer is the commissioner of a big dynasty league: 20 to 32+ teams, often two conferences or twin player pools, often with contracts and defensive players, usually on MyFantasyLeague today. They pay about $100-220 a year for power behind a dated interface, and fear of losing league history is what keeps them there. The commissioner buys and moves the league; the owners are the users we must delight on the phone, because they decide whether the league stays and they start the next league.

The door-opener customer is the long-running 10-16 team redraft league just pushed from NFL.com onto ESPN (announced July 2026), or fed up with ESPN or Yahoo. They come for history import and a better experience; some become keeper or dynasty leagues later. Pitch: "you already moved once; move to something better."

Smaller dynasty and redraft leagues are served well but are not who we design around first. Streamers and content creators are welcome, not a target.

**Why.** No app does everything well; serious leagues patch together a host, trade values, spreadsheets, a group chat and a payment app. Big leagues are the future of the game and the worst served. Research: `docs/research/icp-1122/`.

**What it means for agents.** "Everything" means the whole patched-together stack for these leagues: host, trade values, contracts, history, chat; dues are tracked and reminded, never held or moved by TheWarRoom. Competitors' signature strengths are requirements before outside leagues join: Sleeper-speed live scores and alerts, the full lineup on one phone screen, automatic stat corrections that show what changed, and MFL-level settings and history. The user interface decides whether people stay; "good enough" is not good enough.
