# The app brings information forward by moment; the Today tab is home

Status: Accepted by Christopher
Date: 2026-11-22

**Decision.** The phone is designed around the owner's moments of the week (Tuesday waivers, midweek news, the game-day sweat, the dynasty off-season), not by shrinking the desktop. League type decides what each moment shows. One Today tab covers all of a user's leagues, ranked by what is most urgent, and is where the app opens, except at moments like five minutes to kickoff, when it opens on live games. Commissioner nudges (sign-ups, dues, inactive owners) live on Today.

**Why.** The desktop is close to the best in the business; the phone is not close. Today is our stickiness nexus.

**What it means for agents.** A phone market study of Sleeper, ESPN and Yahoo (read-only) and two or three mock directions on a real phone start after December 6. The Today tab gets its own session: thorough, chained research on player psychology, complaints, and why people love, keep or delete apps, before any design. P07 stays in force until the new mocks are approved.
