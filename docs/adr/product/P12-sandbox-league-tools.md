# Leagues get a sandbox of tools, with smart defaults and wizards

Status: Accepted by Christopher
Date: 2026-11-22

**Decision.** TheWarRoom houses leagues as a sandbox, not a rigid box. Rules sit one tap away from the moment they matter, not printed beside everything. Votes, a versioned constitution and sign-to-join are tools the league switches on. Every trade shows a value scale; the commissioner picks the review style (redraft defaults to none, dynasty defaults to review on and asks how many reviewers), and gets a private heads-up on suspicious trades, never an automatic block. Lore is fully customizable: automatic awards plus commissioner-made trophies that pass down like engraved plates, a Tuesday-morning recap in a voice the commissioner picks, two power rankings for dynasty, owner profiles, and later league reskins. Mini-games are switched on per league and pay rewards in league currency, never real money.

**Why.** Commissioners skew older and may never use the AI path; the hard part of the job must come off their plate. Make it so good that owners start their own league if the commissioner quits.

**What it means for agents.** Setup is a choose-your-own-adventure wizard per league preset; "do your owners know each other?" comes early and shapes the defaults. Wizards come after a tested foundation, in this order: import and new league, off-season rollover, owner replacement, then the rest. Every wizard step issues the same commands as settings and the AI. The fun layer follows the seven rules in `docs/research/social-moments-2026-11-08.md`, with real-player photos allowed per P05.
