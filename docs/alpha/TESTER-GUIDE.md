# Alpha tester guide — phone

**This is the preproduction lab.** Seats open Saturday 11-21. Testing starts Sunday 11-22.
Wait for the commissioner to say a seat is ready before you claim a team.
Use only the sign-in or join link they give you. Do not use your MFL password or forward a join link.
Keep your password and recovery codes private. The server needs an internet connection.

## Open The War Room

1. On your phone, open [The War Room](https://twr-lab-260.secureprospective.com/app/) in Chrome.
   The first screen is **Sign in**. Enter your username and password. Enter an authenticator code if asked.
2. To install it:
   - **Android:** In Chrome, tap **⋮**, then **Install app**, then **Install/Add**.
   - **iPhone:** In Chrome, tap **Share** by the address bar, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open the new **The War Room** icon to return to the app.
   If you sign out, sign in again with the same account.

## What you will see on a phone

On a phone (screen narrower than 700 px) the app shows the phone layout.

- **Bottom:** five tabs: **Today**, **Live**, **Team**, **Moves**, **League**.
- **Top left:** the league name and week button. Tap it to switch leagues.
  The sheet also has **Authenticator** and **Sign out** at its foot.
- **Top right:** **Ask**.

On a computer the desktop layout is unchanged.

## If you need to claim a team

Ask the commissioner for a join link. Each join link works once. After you use it, it is spent.
Open the link, tap **Get started**, choose a team shown as open, enter a sign-in name and a password
of at least 10 characters, then tap **Claim my team**. Save the recovery codes shown once.
Then open The War Room above and sign in.
If the link fails, ask the commissioner for a new one. Do not make another account.
If no team is open, stop and ask the commissioner.

No offline league data is available. If a screen says “Not available yet,” note the screen,
your phone and your browser.
