# Alpha tester guide — phone

**This is the preproduction lab.** The league currently has no open teams; wait for the commissioner to say a seat is ready before claiming one. Use only the sign-in or private join link they give you. Do not use your MFL password or forward a join link. Keep your password and recovery codes private. The server needs an internet connection.

## Open The War Room

1. On your phone, open [The War Room](https://twr-lab-260.secureprospective.com/app/) in Chrome. The first screen is **Sign in**. Enter your The War Room username and password; enter an authenticator code if asked. After sign-in, you should see **Home**, your league card and shortcuts such as **Franchise HQ**, **War Room** and **Pulse**. Commissioners also see **Control Room**.
2. To install it:
   - **Android:** In Chrome, tap **⋮** → **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**) → **Install/Add**.
   - **iPhone:** In Chrome, tap **Share** by the address bar → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
3. Open the new **The War Room** icon to return to the app. If you sign out, sign in again with the same account.

## If you need to claim a team

Ask the commissioner for a private join link first. Open it, tap **Get started**, choose a team shown as open, enter a sign-in name and a password of at least 10 characters, then tap **Claim my team**. Save the recovery codes shown once. Then open The War Room above and sign in. The page says a link works once, but the current server may keep it live for other open teams: never forward it, and wait for the commissioner to replace it. If no team is open or the link is refused, stop and ask the commissioner; do not make another account.

**Phone limitation:** Some league screens are clipped on a 390-pixel phone display, and some league reads may say “Not available yet.” If content is missing or cut off, note the screen and phone/browser; use a larger display for the full screen rather than guessing at an action. No offline league data is available.
