## Delivered
- MFL is ignored by both raw-field merging and automatic revision selection. No MFL acquisition or
  league-origin branch added. Sleeper runs on scoreboard/identity outages and on failed summary acquisition;
  both providers failing retain the prior book. A partially failed ESPN acquisition cannot replace a held
  book unless the independent Sleeper acquisition succeeds.
- Fixed an acquisition gap: `livegame.RawBox` now validates and retains fumbles, interceptions, kicking,
  punting and return categories rather than silently dropping them before conversion.
- ESPN now distinguishes kick/punt/interception return TDs; carries kicking/punting totals and combined
  PAT misses/blocks; converts field-goal distances/bands only when uniquely identified play descriptions
  reconcile exactly with the kicker's box totals. Narrow, explicit goal-line recovery descriptions distinguish
  offensive from opponent-recovery TDs; ambiguous names, incomplete plays and ambiguous recovery prose stay absent.
- Sleeper's live struct whitelist is replaced by 86 numeric protocol-field mappings plus combined kick-band
  mappings, including first downs, red-zone work, rushing TFL, return/recovery TDs, kicking, ratios and edge
  metrics. Every individual map entry is exercised against the league dictionary. Sparse zero bins are accepted
  only when their sum reconciles with the supplied kick total. No invented exact kick or touchdown distances.
  Legacy pinned decoding is unchanged.
- `source.ConsolidateFinal` appends a distinct merged revision: actual nflverse fields win; latest live fields
  fill gaps; older held Sleeper fills are not promoted to nflverse authority. Derived fields never become final
  authority on the next merge. Original revisions are not rewritten. Digest includes constituent revisions,
  values and ownership; attribution changes invalidate live digests without changing legacy `LineDigest`.
- `live_revisions.go` retains the in-process chronology across polls/final arrivals, so a manual pin to an
  earlier merged revision remains available. Retained last-live evidence fills a final even after the active
  board clears. The worker and score reading see the same revision/digest. `heldSeason` was not restructured;
  `native_stats.go` only attaches the live/merged overlay after its existing book construction.
- Per-player source and per-field owners are serialized by `NativePlayerScore` and results starters. Labels:
  `live · ESPN`, `filled · Sleeper`, `final · nflverse`, `commish override`. Commissioner pins/corrections and
  point adjustments are labelled. The score strip aggregates actual selected sources, not policy; the starter
  inspector includes field attribution. Wails models were regenerated by `make build`, not hand-edited.

## No checked live supply / coverage debt
Do not equate “not mapped” with “the upstream API cannot ever carry it.” A direct, profile-independent
Sleeper 2025/week-1 contract check was attempted once and returned HTTP 403; no response was imported.
The captured Sleeper fixture verifies offense/edge keys, not an exhaustive IDP/kicking schema.

The implemented live chain cannot supply complete per-play lists for long passes/rushes/catches, most
TD/return/recovery distances, third/fourth-down conversions, or offensive-line unit quantities. It also
cannot reliably separate blocked FG/punt/PAT totals, own-recovery yardage, defensive vs special-teams
fumble components, missed-FG/blocked-kick return TDs, pick-sixes thrown, one-point safeties or conversion
returns. Team-defense/rank/time-of-possession metrics are not joined to player weeks. These fields remain
absent, awaiting checked nflverse evidence or an explicit commissioner entry; absence is never zero.
Position-specific tackle/assist aliases are engine rate choices, not separate provider observations.
Ambiguous ESPN recovery prose deliberately falls through to Sleeper, then nflverse.

## Remaining debt / next move
1. Live evidence and the merged chronology are **in process only**, like the original live book. Archived
   finals replay unchanged, but a restart cannot replay a pin/automatic approval to a live-derived revision
   from the database alone. Durable capture/append storage is required before claiming full immutable live
   replay or restart-safe live-only gap retention. No database schema or held-season cache was introduced
   silently; ClaudeBox should own that storage decision and its restart tests.
2. Exhaustive upstream field coverage is **not established** by synthetic mapping tests. Obtain checked
   full Sleeper IDP/kicker responses and ESPN recovery variants; verify keys/semantics and extend the narrow
   conversion where justified. Do not certify every possible league stat as live-covered from this report.
3. Reconcile the inherited bloat baseline through the integration owner, without raising it or padding code.
4. Christopher's eventual disposable-profile check: with ESPN denied and Sleeper supplied, watch a running
   score; post a final lacking a live-only field, inspect its retained value/source, then pin/correct it and
   verify later polls cannot replace the pin. Restart preservation is explicitly not claimed.
