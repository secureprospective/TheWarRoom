## KEY FINDINGS (drive the C-2 weight schemes)

1. **Madden is a strong, stable quality signal at EVERY position** — MaddenComposite tracks MaddenOverall
   r≈0.9+ at offense/CB/S, looser at RB/LB (0.68/0.78). Distributions are tight and well-behaved
   (offense p50 72–79, IDP p50 73–75). It earns the promoted-regulator seat (α≈0.20) and can carry more.
2. **NFLProduction is a VOLUME/role signal, NOT a quality signal — and it partially double-counts Madden.**
   Extreme right-skew (QB p50 291 → p75 2070 → p95 4319: starters vs backups, not skill), and it
   correlates r≈0.6–0.84 with Madden at offense. → **Confirms the cap mandate**: uncapped it would inject
   playing-time into a *scouting* rank AND re-weight what Madden already says. Recommend a hard cap +
   treat as a low-weight tiebreaker, never a primary.
3. **Madden man/zone coverage are near-collinear (r≈0.85–0.95 at every position).** A coverage composite
   must NOT count both as independent inputs — average them into one coverage sub-attr.
4. **pfr CoverRatingAllowed is INDEPENDENT of Madden coverage grades (|r|<0.30 at CB/S).** This is the most
   important IDP finding: PFR passer-rating-allowed carries information Madden does NOT → Coverage is a
   *genuine* additive signal at CB/S, not a Madden restatement. It justifies a real (not token) weight.
   Note the raw scale is "allowed" (LOWER is better) → engine must invert on normalize.
5. **RAS_v1 is modestly correlated with Madden (r≈0.3–0.48) — complementary, not redundant.** Athletic
   testing is partly baked into Madden but adds signal; the existing RAS weight need not change for FILM.
6. **VetFilm (FTN) sign-checks correct.** QB primary = InterceptionWorthyRate (lower=better) → its
   NEGATIVE correlation with Madden/production (r≈−0.45) is the RIGHT direction. WR primary =
   ContestedCatchRate (p50 0.17). Coverage is thin (n=38–94 above floor) → FTN is a *veteran-only*,
   lower-population anchor; Madden must fall back for the rest. Supports "FTN primary + Madden fallback"
   with an FTN fidelity discount, but the low n means FTN cannot be the sole veteran film driver.

### Methodology caveats (honest limits of this sheet)

- **CollegeShare correlation NOT captured** — structural, not a bug. College-2023 vs Madden-m24 (2023 NFL
  season) have near-zero player overlap (a player is in college OR the NFL that year). College resolved
  895 gsis cleanly but doesn't intersect the current-NFL film population. A real college-vs-film
  double-count check needs the *windowed pre-NFL* college pull (as BreakoutAge does) — DEFERRED as heavy
  + secondary. The film-vs-RAS check covers the wired-signal double-count concern for now.
- **Season mix** — signals are from adjacent-but-different seasons (m24 / 2024 / 2025-FTN→re-run 2024).
  Fine for rank-correlation + distribution shape; not a within-season point estimate.
- **CFBD key hygiene (checked, OK)** — `CFBD_API_KEY` on CT105 carries a trailing newline; the sampler
  initially failed on it. Production is NOT affected: `m1_scouting.go:73` already `TrimSpace`s the key
  (documented in `ingestion/cfbd.go`). No action needed.
