# TheWarRoom Licensing — Plain-English Summary

**Last updated: 2026-07-11**

This document explains, in layman's terms, how TheWarRoom is licensed and owned,
and why it's set up this way. It is a plain-English companion to the legal files
(`LICENSE`, `NOTICE`, `CLA.md`, and `docs/licensing/TFM-Grant.md`) — the legal
files control if there's ever a conflict.

## The one-sentence version

**TheWarRoom is free for anyone to use and tinker with non-commercially, forever;
it is owned entirely by SecureProspective LLC; nobody but SecureProspective may
sell or commercialize it; and Tech Freedom Ministries has a permanent, free,
unchangeable right to use it however it wishes.**

## The key idea: ownership ≠ license

Two separate things:

- **Ownership (copyright)** = *who owns it.* → 100% SecureProspective LLC (Texas).
- **License** = *a permission slip handed to others* saying what they may do.

Because SecureProspective owns the copyright, it can hand out different
permission slips to different people at the same time. That's how all of the
below is true simultaneously.

## What each party can do

| Who | What they may do |
|---|---|
| **The public / homelabbers** | Run, fork, modify, self-host, study, and share — **free, for any non-commercial purpose, forever.** |
| **Tech Freedom Ministries** | Everything the public can do, **plus commercial use** — permanently and for free. Cannot be taken away, even after a sale. |
| **SecureProspective LLC** | Owns it outright. Can sell it, license it commercially, and set all terms. |
| **Anyone wanting commercial use** | Must get a commercial license from SecureProspective. |

## Is this "open source"? No — and that's deliberate

"Open source" (the OSI definition) *requires* allowing commercial use by anyone.
TheWarRoom does not, so the honest label is **"source-available, non-commercial."**
The code is public and free to play with; it just isn't free to *sell*.

## The four documents

1. **`LICENSE`** — [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](../../LICENSE). The
   public license: free non-commercial use for everyone.
2. **`NOTICE`** — records copyright ownership (SecureProspective LLC) and the
   standing TFM grant.
3. **`CLA.md`** — the Contributor License Agreement. Anyone who contributes code
   grants SecureProspective a broad license to it (contributors keep their own
   copyright). This keeps ownership consolidated so the Project can be maintained
   and, if the right offer comes, sold as one clean asset.
4. **`docs/licensing/TFM-Grant.md`** — Tech Freedom Ministries' permanent,
   irrevocable, free-use grant, written to survive any change of ownership.

## Why it's built this way

- **"People play, not profit."** Free non-commercial use maximizes reach and
  goodwill; the commercial reservation protects the asset's value.
- **The door to a sale stays open.** SecureProspective owns everything and holds
  broad rights to all contributions, so it can sell the whole Project later if a
  buyer makes the right offer. (This is also why the license does *not*
  auto-convert to free open source over time — that would destroy sale value.)
- **TFM is protected forever.** TFM inspired the Project; its free-use rights are
  permanent and clamp onto any future owner. A buyer inherits the code *with*
  TFM's rights attached and cannot strip them.

## Not legal advice

The `LICENSE` file is a standard, ready-to-use license. The **CLA** and the
**TFM grant** are contracts tailored to SecureProspective's goals and should be
reviewed by a qualified Texas attorney before they are relied upon, especially
given the commercial and potential-sale objectives.
