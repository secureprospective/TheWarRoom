# Standing License Grant to Tech Freedom Ministries

**Version 1.0 — 2026**

> ⚠️ **Draft for attorney review.** This is the plain-language grant that
> records Tech Freedom Ministries' permanent rights in TheWarRoom. Because it is
> intended to bind future owners and to survive a sale, have a qualified Texas
> attorney review and countersign it before relying on it.

## Parties

- **Grantor / Owner:** SecureProspective LLC, a Texas limited liability company
  ("SecureProspective").
- **Grantee:** Tech Freedom Ministries, and its successor 501(c)(3) entity upon
  formation ("TFM").

## Recitals

TheWarRoom was created with Tech Freedom Ministries as its founding inspiration.
In recognition of that, SecureProspective grants TFM the permanent rights set
out below. These rights are a condition of the Project's existence and are not
subject to renegotiation.

## The Grant

SecureProspective hereby grants to TFM a **perpetual, irrevocable,
royalty-free, worldwide, non-exclusive license** to use, run, copy, modify,
and internally distribute TheWarRoom (the "Software") **for any purpose,
including commercial use,** in furtherance of TFM's mission, as TFM sees fit.

## Permanence and Survival — This Cannot Be Taken Away

1. **Irrevocable and perpetual.** This grant may not be revoked, suspended, or
   diminished by SecureProspective for any reason.
2. **Binding on successors and assigns.** This grant runs with the Software and
   is binding on SecureProspective's successors, assigns, and any future owner
   of the Software or of SecureProspective. **A purchaser or transferee of the
   Software or of SecureProspective acquires it subject to this grant and
   cannot alter, reduce, or terminate TFM's rights.**
3. **Condition of any transfer.** SecureProspective shall not sell, assign, or
   otherwise transfer the Software or its copyright except on the express
   written condition that the acquirer assumes and honors this grant in full.
4. **No cost, ever.** TFM owes SecureProspective nothing — no fee, royalty, or
   other consideration — for the rights granted here, now or in the future.

## What This Grant Is Not

This grant does **not** transfer ownership of the Software. Copyright in the
Software remains solely with SecureProspective (or its lawful successor). TFM
receives a permanent license to *use* the Software, not title to it.

## Governing Law

This grant is governed by the laws of the State of Texas, USA.
