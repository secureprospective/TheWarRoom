- Join: PFR player ID → DynastyProcess `pfr_id` → MFL `mfl_id`; GSIS bridge if needed. Do not collapse MFL's duplicate IDs blindly.

**Candidate: NFL official injury report. Already approved.** https://www.nfl.com/injuries/ retrieved **“Week 4 of the 2026 Season”** and team/player injury tables. All roster positions, current weekly report; no 2021 history guarantee or numeric refresh SLA established here. Free HTML/no login; NFL terms, no bulk-reuse licence established. Page links/names require verified NFL/GSIS mapping or manual name+team reconciliation. Use for current designation corroboration, not automated historical missed-game causality.

**Candidate: nflverse participation + roster history for observed availability. Needs Christopher's approval.** https://nflreadr.nflverse.com/reference/load_participation.html and https://nflreadr.nflverse.com/articles/dictionary_participation.html retrieved. N1: pre-2023 NFL NGS source died during 2023; FTN participation from 2023 is released **after all postseason games**, NOT during season. Not a verified all-reasons injury-absence label, and not a live denominator for current pressures/routes. FTN redistribution conditions require dataset-specific check; do not assume all charting/participation licences identical.

## G5: history back to 2021
- **Confirmed season asset existence:** PBP 2021 and snap_counts 2021 in release JSON, and 2026 equivalents (Sector 1). PBP history is documented since 1999; snaps since 2012. Multi-season sums are possible without discarding injured veterans' earlier evidence.
- **Current player-stats migration:** current `stats_player` release includes modern combined offense/defense/kicking; `stats_player_reg_2021.csv` exists in fetched release metadata, updated 2026-08-13T16:48:29Z (911478 bytes). Row-level field completeness still requires inspection before calling every historical IDP field complete. Old offense-only files are insufficient evidence of missing defensive history in the new format.
- **Injury history:** loader documents since 2009 and current release explicitly includes 2024/2025/2026. Historical reported status is not a causal missed-games table.
- **PFR advanced stats:** documented since 2018, with some season-only fields. Exact defender field/year completeness could not be established with this decoder.
- **FTN charting:** starts 2022, so it CANNOT supply 2021. Participation changes provider in 2023; treat source regime changes explicitly.
- **Routes / run stops / coverage opportunities:** no complete free 2021–2026 table established. Do not fabricate zero values for unavailable history, or treat absence as poor play.
