Which values deserve a second look?

Current engine, held Legacy NFL data. Read-only candidates for review — not proof that a dynasty value is wrong, and not a new ranking model.

This is an archived early-season snapshot, not live results. No live fetch was attempted.

@@PERIOD@@

Players ranked by dynasty points per game, highest first. Compare the value rank with actual points rank; open a player’s inputs to see what the engine used.

@@CHECKS@@
Checks, data dates & missing outcomes

@@PROVENANCE@@

@@POOL@@ Actual ranks use this season’s total league fantasy points among the same valued players; tied scores share a rank. Missing is “—”, not zero. Ages are on September 1 of the valued season, matching the engine.

The points-per-game rank helps separate injury or playing-time effects from talent. League contracts are context only: these engine values do not include salary or positional scarcity adjustments.

@@COVERAGE@@. Missing totals never trigger a points comparison; they are not treated as a score of zero.

Rank gap: at least 20 places between dynasty rank and current total-points rank, with either rank in the top 20 and a known current total (including an explicit zero).

Zero-valued producer: a current top-20 scorer with positive points but zero dynasty value.

Older over producer: a top-30 valued older player above someone at least four years younger who scored over 25% more in both seasons. Older starts at QB 34, RB 29, WR 31, TE 32, K 36, DT/DE 31, LB/CB 30, S 31. These are review thresholds, not fitted age curves. One younger example is shown per flagged older player.

Counts overlap. Neither a short season nor a lower dynasty value proves a defect. No run-jump check: stored runs changed engine and parameters, so their differences do not isolate an unexplained jump. No cross-position check: lineup scarcity is not part of these player values.
